## The Event

**Semana da Computação** is a student-organized academic event at the University of São Paulo, created and run by Computer Science undergraduates. It promotes scientific and technological knowledge through talks, workshops, student-designed activities, and interactions with academic and industry professionals.

Its themes include Artificial Intelligence, Data Science, automation, software development, and other areas of computing. The event connects students, researchers, and technology organizations, creating opportunities for knowledge sharing, professional development, networking, and collaboration.

## My Role as General Director

I led the planning and direction of the seventh edition of SemComp over approximately one year. The role involved coordinating people, schedules, external partners, and deliverables while keeping the event aligned with its educational mission.

My main responsibilities included:

- **Planning and execution:** Structured the event schedule and oversaw more than 10 online and in-person activities, including talks, workshops, and student-led sessions.
- **Team leadership:** Coordinated approximately 20 undergraduate organizers across three teams: **Communication, Events, and Marketing**.
- **Delivery management:** Followed progress, organized recurring meetings, managed deadlines, and reviewed materials before final approval.
- **Partnerships and sponsorships:** Prospected organizations, negotiated sponsorship arrangements, and secured approximately **BRL 5,000** in sponsorships.
- **Stakeholder communication:** Worked with students, speakers, university representatives, and companies to support event delivery.

## Sponsorships & External Relations

The seventh edition received sponsorship support from **Alura, Alliage, Smar APD, and Evertec**. I was responsible for prospecting, negotiating, and closing sponsorship agreements, balancing partner expectations with the event's academic objectives.

This work developed my experience in professional communication, negotiation, prioritization, and accountability for measurable results.

## Web Platform & Digital Operations

The organization also involved maintaining event-related digital infrastructure. The web platform helped communicate the schedule and manage participation workflows, including registration-related functionality and organizational data.

My technical contribution and coordination touched web technologies such as **WordPress, PHP, MySQL, HTML, CSS, and JavaScript**. The platform work complemented the event's broader management responsibilities rather than being the sole focus of the project.

## What This Project Demonstrates

SemComp VII was an opportunity to apply technical and organizational skills in a real, time-constrained environment. The experience involved managing concurrent workstreams, coordinating a multidisciplinary student team, maintaining external relationships, and making decisions that affected a large audience.

Beyond the technical platform, the project strengthened my ability to **lead teams, manage scope and deadlines, communicate with stakeholders, and deliver under constraints**.

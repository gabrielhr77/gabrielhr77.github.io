I am an undergraduate Computer Science student at the **University of São Paulo (USP), Ribeirão Preto**, with interests in Artificial Intelligence, Machine Learning, Data Science, and software engineering. My work combines Python and PyTorch-based experimentation, numerical simulations, and web development.

I am particularly interested in opportunities where I can contribute to a development team, learn from experienced engineers, and apply rigorous problem-solving to real products.

## Research Experience

In my **FAPESP-supported undergraduate research**, I explored autoregressive Transformer models and physics-informed training for gravitational two-body and three-body dynamics. I implemented numerical simulations, built datasets, and evaluated predicted trajectories against reference solutions.

[Read the research overview]({{ '/research/' | relative_url }}) · [Explore the code](https://github.com/gabrielhr77/scientific_initiation)

## Leadership & Impact

As **General Director of VII Semana da Computação (SemComp VII)**, I coordinated a year-long organization effort at USP Ribeirão Preto, leading approximately **20 students across three teams**. Together, we delivered **more than 10 in-person and online activities**, secured **BRL 5,000 in sponsorships**, and achieved an **estimated audience reach of 1,000–2,000 viewers**.
